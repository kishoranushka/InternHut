## Worklog
- At the start of every session, read `worklog.md` (repo root) before exploring the code. It is the running record of what was built, why, and what is still open.
- At the end of every session, append a dated `## YYYY-MM-DD — title` section to the end of `worklog.md` covering: files changed, what was verified and how, and open follow-ups. Keep it factual and short. Git history already records the diffs, so the worklog only needs the decisions and context behind them.
